## interior.dev

The widget's micro-interactions (press depth, hold to confirm, long press, segmented control,
slider detents, new items pill, blur-up image) are re-implemented in plain CSS/JavaScript after the
behaviour of the React components from **interior.dev** – https://github.com/ddoemonn/interior.
No source code was copied; motion values and interaction rules follow those components.
